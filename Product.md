Vision for the Expense App

This application is primarily an Expense Management App for individuals and small business owners. Its core purpose is to help users record, organize, and understand their expenses.

Unlike traditional expense trackers that only record amounts spent, this app provides context, analytics, and planning tools to help users make better financial decisions.

The expense tracker remains the core of the application. Every additional feature should enhance the expense management experience rather than replace it.

⸻

Core Features

1. Expense Tracking (Core)

Users can:

* Record expenses quickly.
* Organize expenses into profiles (Personal, Business, etc.).
* Categorize expenses.
* Add notes, receipts, and attachments.
* Filter and search expenses.
* View expense history.

⸻

2. Smart Categories

Each expense category can have its own custom fields.

Examples:

Fuel

* Amount
* Litres
* Price per litre
* Fuel station
* Vehicle
* Odometer

Eating Out

* Restaurant
* Meal type
* Number of people

Electricity

* Amount
* kWh consumed

Internet

* Provider
* Package
* Data usage (optional)

Business Advertising

* Campaign
* Platform
* Leads generated
* Cost per lead

The app should support category-specific data instead of using the same fields for every expense.

⸻

3. Analytics

Users should understand more than just how much they spent.

Examples:

Fuel

* Total litres this month
* Total amount spent
* Average price per litre
* Monthly trend

Food

* Meals purchased
* Average meal cost
* Monthly spending trend

Business

* Operating costs
* Marketing spend
* Software subscriptions
* Cost by department

⸻

4. Budgets

Users can create monthly budgets for:

* Categories
* Profiles
* Entire businesses

The app tracks progress and alerts users when they approach or exceed their budget.

⸻

5. Planner (Additional Feature)

The Planner is not a replacement for expense tracking.

It is an additional feature that helps users estimate future expenses before they happen.

Examples:

* If I spend TSh 5,000 every day on lunch, how much will I spend this month?
* If I drive 60 km daily, what will my monthly fuel cost be?
* If I subscribe to these services, what will my yearly cost be?
* If I reduce my daily coffee spending, how much could I save?

The Planner should include:

* Daily habit calculator
* Fuel cost calculator
* Subscription planner
* Savings simulator
* Business operating cost estimator

These are planning tools that complement the recorded expense data.

⸻

6. Reports

Generate reports by:

* Day
* Week
* Month
* Year
* Custom date range

Users can filter reports by:

* Profile
* Category
* Business
* Payment method
* Tags

⸻

7. Insights

The app should automatically generate useful insights based on recorded expenses.

Examples:

* You spent 15% more on fuel than last month.
* Eating out increased by 8 meals this month.
* Fuel prices increased by TSh 100/L.
* Software subscriptions now account for 22% of your business expenses.
* Your largest expense category this month was marketing.

The goal is to help users understand spending patterns, not just view numbers.

⸻

Product Philosophy

This should feel like an Expense Intelligence App, not just an expense tracker.

The app should answer questions such as:

* Where is my money going?
* Why did I spend more this month?
* Which category is increasing over time?
* How much does my lifestyle cost every month?
* How much does each of my businesses cost to operate?
* What will I spend if I continue this habit?
* How can I reduce my expenses?

Every new feature should support this philosophy by making expenses easier to record, understand, analyze, and plan for.
